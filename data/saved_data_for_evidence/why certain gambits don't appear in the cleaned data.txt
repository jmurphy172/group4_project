Why do the above Gambits not appear in the cleaned data?

	drop_duplicates() first removes every game after the first time a “White” player appears, then removes every game after the first time a “Black” player appears.

	In effect we keep at most one game per player, total.

	If the only occurrence of the Tal Gambit involves a player who had an earlier game in the file, that row is discarded and never reaches the cleaning stage.
